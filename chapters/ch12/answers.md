# 第 12 章「饮食健康」参考答案与用法提醒

答案按卡号排列。开放表达可以使用其他意思相符、表达自然的句子。

## 01 · 在餐馆点餐

I usually order noodles when I eat out.

order a meal 是点餐，a takeaway 是一份外带餐。

## 02 · cuisine

Visitors can sample the local cuisine at the night market.

cuisine 通常指一种菜系或烹饪风格。

## 03 · 午餐怎么选

My diet includes several vegetarian meals each week.

food 指食物，diet 可指平时吃什么。

## 04 · 厨师照着食谱备菜

I followed a recipe for tomato soup.

edible 表示能吃。a recipe for soup 是汤的食谱。

## 05 · 在学校和旅馆取餐

The buffet had fruit, bread and hot dishes.

cafeteria 指场所，buffet 可指一种供餐方式。

## 06 · 社区聚餐怎么安排

A family-run restaurant catered for our school event.

treat someone to lunch 表示请某人吃午饭。

## 07 · 三种聚餐方式

We held a barbecue to celebrate my friend's birthday.

banquet 往往较正式，supper 指晚餐。

## 08 · 小食在什么时候吃

I'd order a small salad as an appetiser.

refreshments 常用复数。appetiser 是餐前小菜。

## 09 · gourmet

I'd like to try the gourmet food at the local food festival.

gourmet 也可作形容词，如 gourmet food。

## 10 · 餐桌上的刀叉勺

I eat grilled fish with a knife and fork.

cutlery 通常不可数，可说 a set of cutlery。

## 11 · 餐具是什么材质

My grandmother gave me a small porcelain cup.

porcelain 是陶瓷的一种，常译作瓷器。

## 12 · 托盘上的杯碟和碗

I had a bowl of porridge for breakfast.

dish 可指盘子，也可指一道菜。

## 13 · 茶水间里倒一杯

I usually have a glass of water when I wake up.

a glass of water 和 a mug of tea 都可表示一杯的量。

## 14 · 炉子上的两种锅

I heat a pan before adding the eggs.

frying pan 常用于煎，a pot of soup 表示一锅汤。

## 15 · 面包房里的两种炉子

I would bake a loaf of bread in the oven.

oven 用来烘烤，furnace 可为建筑供暖。

## 16 · 打开食品罐

I bought a tin of tomatoes for the soup.

a tin of peas 是一罐豌豆。

## 17 · 咖啡馆里的饮料选择

I usually drink coffee with breakfast.

drink 也可作名词。beverage 较正式。

## 18 · 展柜里的酒精与烈酒

Brandy contains alcohol.

alcohol 可指酒精，也可指酒类。

## 19 · 聚餐结束后怎么回家

She stayed sober and called a taxi for her friends.

stay sober 表示保持清醒、未醉酒。

## 20 · 从烟叶到卷烟

People should not smoke cigarettes at bus stops.

tobacco 指烟草，a cigarette 指一支香烟。

## 21 · 菜摊上的红紫绿

I would make a salad with tomatoes and cucumbers.

eggplant 在英式英语中也叫 aubergine。

## 22 · 从菜地收上来的四种菜

We pulled up a few radishes from the garden.

potato 是块茎，turnip 和 radish 是根菜。

## 23 · 给一锅汤备料

I would add cabbage and an onion to the soup.

peas 和 beans 常用复数指多颗食材。

## 24 · 果摊上的果串与小果

I like fresh fruit, including grapes and papayas.

泛指水果常用 fruit，说种类也可用 fruits。

## 25 · 果园里摘下的三种水果

I would pick a few ripe peaches.

pick fruit 在这里指从树上采摘水果。

## 26 · 切开橙子、柠檬和甜瓜

I would add a slice of lemon to sparkling water.

一片柠檬可说 a slice of lemon。

## 27 · 去皮、剥去、去核与去壳

First, peel the apple. Then remove its core.

peel 常指去果皮，hull 可指去谷粒外壳。

## 28 · kiwi

I cut a ripe kiwi in half and eat it with a spoon.

指这种水果时，也可以说 kiwifruit。

## 29 · 从麦田到一把麦粒

The farmer is using a sickle to reap the wheat crop.

crop 指庄稼。grain 可指一粒谷物。

## 30 · 厨房里的面粉、粥与糊

I make porridge with oats and top it with apple slices.

flour 是粉末。paste 指黏稠的糊。

## 31 · 农场里的家畜与家禽

The farm raises chickens and turkeys.

livestock 表集合。chicken 和 turkey 也可指肉。

## 32 · 菜市场怎么选肉

I usually buy pork to make dumplings.

mutton 通常指成年羊肉。lamb 指羔羊肉。

## 33 · 在池塘边钓鱼

I can see two fish near the edge of the pond.

钓鱼时常说 fishing rod。

## 34 · 乳品柜里有什么

I often buy dairy products such as milk and plain yogurt.

dairy products 指乳制品。yogurt 也可拼 yoghurt。

## 35 · 早餐桌上的面包与抹酱

I like to spread butter and strawberry jam on my toast.

loaf 是一整条。单片用 a slice of bread。

## 36 · 甜点柜里的三种点心

I like biscuits with a crisp texture.

图中 biscuit 是英式用法，指饼干。

## 37 · 午餐柜台怎么点餐

Could I have a hamburger and a salad, please?

点餐可用 Could I have …, please?

## 38 · 意面与比萨怎么区分

I usually order spaghetti with tomato sauce.

pasta 是总称。spaghetti 指细长圆条意面。

## 39 · 冰淇淋柜台的三种选择

I would like an ice cream with chopped nuts on top.

ice cream 可指食物，也可说 an ice cream 指一份。

## 40 · 香草的两种用途

Eggs are the main ingredient in my omelette.

ingredient 指构成食品或产品的原料。

## 41 · 市集上的香料与芥末

I mix a little mustard with freshly ground pepper.

pepper 在这里指胡椒。wasabi 是山葵。

## 42 · 炒菜前备好葱姜蒜

I use garlic and ginger in my vegetable stir-fry.

scallion 常见于美式英语，英式常说 spring onion。

## 43 · 调沙拉汁的四样配料

I add a little vinegar and a pinch of salt to the dressing.

a pinch of salt 指一小撮盐。

## 44 · 糖果摊上的两个动作

He licked the chocolate off his spoon.

suck on a sweet 指含着糖果吮吸。

## 45 · 同一张桌上的四种味道

The lemon sauce tasted sour and a little sweet.

flavour 是英式拼写，美式写作 flavor。

## 46 · 一道菜好吃，怎么说

The vegetable soup was delicious, especially with fresh herbs.

三个词都可夸食物好吃，yummy 更口语化。

## 47 · 徒步歇脚时的饥与渴

I felt thirsty after cycling up the hill.

hunger 是名词，hungry 和 thirsty 是形容词。

## 48 · 食物入口后的三个动作

First we chew the food, and then we swallow it.

chew gum 指嚼口香糖。digest 强调食物被分解。

## 49 · 把薯条蘸进酱汁

I like to dip warm bread in a herby sauce.

ketchup 是一种 sauce。dip A in B 指把 A 蘸进 B。

## 50 · supplement

According to the label, this is a dietary supplement.

作动词时，supplement 表示补充。

## 51 · 做饭时的三种加热法

I like to fry an egg for breakfast.

bake 常搭配 bread。roast 常搭配 meat 或 vegetables。

## 52 · 早餐台上的三样准备

I soak the oats in water before breakfast.

这里 toast 是不可数名词，可说 a slice of toast。

## 53 · 拌沙拉前的三种动作

First I grind some pepper, and then I stir it into the dressing.

slice the cucumber 指把黄瓜切成薄片。
