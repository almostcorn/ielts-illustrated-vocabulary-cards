# 第 11 章「时尚潮流」参考答案与用法提醒

答案按卡号排列。开放表达可以使用其他意思相符、表达自然的句子。

## 01 · 街头穿搭中的时尚与趋势

I sometimes follow a trend, but I choose clothes that suit my own style.

fashion 说时尚风向，style 说穿搭方式，trend 强调变化趋势。

## 02 · 首饰、宝石与玉石

My grandmother gave me a piece of jewellery with a small jade pendant.

jewellery 不可数。jewel 和 gem 都可指宝石。

## 03 · vogue

Wide-leg jeans are back in vogue in my city.

常用搭配是 in vogue，也可以说 come into vogue。

## 04 · 怎样看出一种穿搭流行

The popularity of oversized jackets has grown in recent years.

tendency 强调倾向，popularity 指受欢迎程度。

## 05 · 秀场模特与人们崇拜的偶像

I consider her a fashion icon because her practical designs changed everyday clothing.

icon 强调代表性，idol 强调崇拜。同一个人可以兼有两种身份。

## 06 · 精品店里的一次购物

Buying three designer coats at once seems extravagant because I only need one warm coat.

luxury 指奢侈品。extravagant 可评价花费过多。

## 07 · 一件节日外套怎么装饰

The tailor is embroidering flowers on the cuffs.

embroider 强调针线。embellish 强调加细节美化。

## 08 · 发廊里的剪发与染发

The stylist used a dye to colour the hair sample.

pigment 是赋予颜色的物质。dye 用来给材料染色。

## 09 · 假面舞会的装扮

I would wear a velvet costume with a long cape.

wear a veil 表示戴面纱。

## 10 · 展柜前为什么停下脚步

The butterfly pattern on the bag fascinated me.

事物 + fascinate + 人。decent 表示达到不错的水准。

## 11 · 上台前的最后一遍走位

She turned and raised her arms with grace.

with grace 形容动作。a perfect fit 指完全合脚。

## 12 · 出门前的梳妆台

I sometimes wear make-up to a formal dinner.

cosmetics 可包括护肤用品。make-up 也可指化好的妆。

## 13 · 布景店里的五种审美

I find the little shopfront charming because of its colourful windows.

handsome 也能夸物件。审美评价会因人而异。

## 14 · 开学前的衣物领取日

A school uniform can save students time when they get ready in the morning.

dress 可指连衣裙。clothe 是动词，指给人提供衣物。

## 15 · 衣服洗好后放哪儿

I put my clean clothes in the wardrobe by the window.

garment 可数。laundry 指衣物时通常不可数。

## 16 · 剧场后台的五件服装

The actress wore a silver wrap over her evening gown.

overall 可指工作罩衣。连体工作服常用 overalls。

## 17 · 冷天怎么叠穿

I wear a thick sweater under my jacket when it gets cold.

图中 vest 用美式义。英式英语常用 waistcoat。

## 18 · 试衣间里选下装

I usually wear a pair of jeans when I go out at weekends.

jeans 和 trousers 常用复数。一条用 a pair of。

## 19 · 外套上的四个细节

The button on the left sleeve needs replacing.

clasp 是搭扣。button 常与扣眼配合。

## 20 · 冬天出门带什么

I usually wear a scarf and gloves on cold mornings.

glove 可数。一双手套用 a pair of gloves。

## 21 · 帽子怎么遮阳

The hat has a wide brim that shades my face.

hat 泛指帽子。图中的 cap 是棒球帽。

## 22 · 市集结账用哪个钱包

I lost a small brown leather wallet.

purse 在英式英语可指零钱包，美式常指手提包。

## 23 · 玄关里换袜穿鞋

I sat by the door to tie my laces before leaving.

lace 在这里指鞋带，也可指花边。

## 24 · 裁缝怎样缝好下摆

The tailor is sewing the hem of a pair of trousers.

sew 强调缝制动作。stitch 作名词可指一个针脚。

## 25 · 给旧牛仔裤打补丁

I would sew a patch over the hole in my jeans.

patch 可以补破洞。rag 常指清洁用的旧布。

## 26 · 走进纺织工坊

The craftsperson uses a spinning wheel to spin wool into yarn.

wool 指纤维。velvet 指有短绒表面的织物。

## 27 · 首饰台上串一颗珠子

I would make a bracelet with blue beads.

string 可指细绳。chain 由一个个链环相连。

## 28 · 包带、条纹与腰带

My bag has a wide strap that makes it comfortable to carry.

strap 是用来系或背的带子。stripe 是条纹。

## 29 · 旧衣试穿与修整

My old jacket looks shabby, so I would like to repair the cuffs.

shabby 描述外观破旧。tight 描述衣物偏紧。

## 30 · 橱窗里的暖色搭配

I often wear a white shirt and carry a brown bag.

colour 是英式拼写，美式写作 color。

## 31 · 试衣镜前的四色搭配

I would wear a grey coat with a purple skirt.

grey 也可拼作 gray。tan 指浅棕色。

## 32 · 褪色、污渍与墨点

My blue shirt has faded after many washes.

fade 是颜色变淡。blot 在图中指纸上的墨点。

## 33 · 裁缝铺的轮廓与微调

I made a slight adjustment to the sleeves of my jacket.

slight 表示程度轻微，常搭配 adjustment。
