# 第 14 章「交通旅行」参考答案与用法提醒

答案按卡号排列。开放表达可以使用其他意思相符、表达自然的句子。

## 01 · 从海上航行到驶入港口

Our voyage ended when the ferry arrived at the island port.

voyage 常指海上长途旅行。

## 02 · aviation

Modern aviation allows people to travel long distances in a short time.

aviation 指航空活动或航空业，通常不可数。

## 03 · 旅程、旅行与观兽之旅

The train journey took three hours. I prefer to travel by rail.

journey 可指一次旅程，travel 也可作动词。

## 04 · 节庆游行吸引了谁

A colourful parade passed through the town centre.

attraction 可指值得去看的地方或活动。

## 05 · haunt

The bookshop near my home is a favourite haunt of mine.

名词 haunt 常用于 a favourite haunt。

## 06 · 旅行中参观纪念建筑

The memorial stands in a quiet garden near the river.

memorial 用于纪念人物或事件，pyramid 指金字塔形建筑。

## 07 · 出发前走进机场

I contacted the airline to change my flight.

airline 指航空公司，flight 可指一次航班。

## 08 · 航空展里的四种辨认

I saw a passenger jet with two large engines.

jet 是喷气式飞机，也属于 airplane。

## 09 · 行李总称与一只箱子

I usually take one small suitcase and a piece of hand luggage.

baggage 和 luggage 通常不可数，可说 a piece of luggage。

## 10 · traffic

Heavy traffic often slows my journey to work.

traffic 通常不可数，可用 heavy 描述车流密集。

## 11 · 上车前收好随身包

I put my carry-on on the overhead rack.

pay the fare 表示支付车费。

## 12 · 货运站里装什么车

The van carried small items of freight to local shops.

lorry 常用于英式英语，truck 在美式英语中常见。

## 13 · 摊开地图规划旅程

The first stop on our itinerary is a small coastal town.

route 是行走路线，itinerary 包含行程安排。

## 14 · 从通道走到路口

Go through the passage and cross the road at the lights.

cross the road 中 cross 是动词。

## 15 · 从小径走向林荫大街

I often walk along the tree-lined avenue near my home.

on the way to 表示在去某处的路上。

## 16 · 公路旁的三处细节

The signpost showed us the way to the station.

curb 是美式拼写，英式常写 kerb。

## 17 · 客运站前的交通工具

I travelled by coach because the tickets were affordable.

vehicle 是车辆的总称。auto 多见于美式用语。

## 18 · 轨道旁的四个词

I would take the tram for a short trip across town.

carriage 指客运车厢，wagon 可指货车厢。

## 19 · 乡间集市的慢行方式

I cycle to the market once a week.

cycle 可作动词，表示骑自行车。

## 20 · 汽修厂里查看动力

I took the car to a garage to have the motor checked.

horsepower 是表示功率的单位。

## 21 · 车轮、轮胎与驾驶动作

The driver braked gently as the traffic slowed.

tyre 是英式拼写，tire 是美式拼写。

## 22 · crew

The crew helped passengers find their seats.

crew 指在飞机或船上共同工作的人员。

## 23 · 船上不同的人做什么

The captain is responsible for the ship and its crew.

sailor 和 seaman 都可指职业水手。

## 24 · 航行中的危险与应急

Ice on a mountain path can be a hazard.

hazard 指可能造成伤害的因素。

## 25 · 碰撞、压坏与猛冲

My bag crashed into the wall as I hurried through the station.

crash 强调碰撞，crush 强调挤压损坏。

## 26 · 船影怎样从视野中消失

The train disappeared into the tunnel.

disappear 较通用，vanish 常强调迅速或突然消失。

## 27 · 港湾、码头与船锚

The old dock is on the east side of the harbour.

harbour 美式拼作 harbor。dock 可指靠船的码头。

## 28 · 海上的三类大船

The large vessel has several decks for passengers.

vessel 是船舶的统称，aircraft carrier 指航空母舰。

## 29 · 从登船到航行

There were only a few passengers aboard that morning.

embark 是登船的动作，aboard 表示在船上。

## 30 · 船尾、甲板与动力

We stood on the deck near the stern.

submerge 表示没入水中，stern 指船尾。

## 31 · 湖面上的小船和筏子

I would like to paddle a canoe on a quiet lake.

canoe 是船，oar 是用来划船的桨。

## 32 · 三种水道怎么辨认

The canal runs beside the old railway.

canal 常指人工运河，ditch 通常较窄较浅。

## 33 · 铁路上的整条线与部件

The railway passes through a valley before reaching the city.

railway 和 railroad 都可指铁路，rail 也可指一根铁轨。

## 34 · 地下列车驶出隧道

I usually take the underground to work.

地铁在美式英语中常说 subway，英式常说 underground。

## 35 · 列车从出发到终点

We departed from the city in the morning and arrived at the coast at noon.

arrive at 后接车站等地点，arrive in 后接城市或国家。

## 36 · 行程推迟了怎么说

We had to defer our departure because of the storm.

postpone 和 defer 后可接名词或动名词。

## 37 · lull

There was a lull in the traffic after the morning rush.

a lull in the traffic 表示交通流量暂时减少。

## 38 · expire

My student travel pass expires at the end of the month.

expire 常用于护照、签证和票券的有效期。

## 39 · 在邮局寄一封信

I put the letter in an envelope and stuck a stamp on it.

mail 作邮件总称时通常不可数。

## 40 · 把物品包装好再寄出

I wrapped the book as a parcel and bound it with string.

parcel 和 package 都可指包裹，packet 常指小包装。

## 41 · 装载、转运与重担

The parcel was transferred from the lorry to a smaller van.

load sth onto a vehicle 表示把物品装上交通工具。

## 42 · 包裹在路上，什么时候到

My new suitcase is due to arrive on Friday.

in transit 表示在运输途中，be due to arrive 表示预计到达。

## 43 · 把信息传给司机

A clear map can convey the route more effectively than a long explanation.

convey 可指运送，也可指传达意思。

## 44 · 看速度，也看方向

The courier made a swift delivery to the office.

物理语境中，velocity 同时包含快慢和方向。
