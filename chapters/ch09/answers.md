# 第 9 章「娱乐运动」参考答案与用法提醒

答案按卡号排列。开放表达可以使用其他意思相符、表达自然的句子。

## 01 · 体育新闻怎样出炉

Radio is a useful medium for following live sport.

medium 指传播渠道，复数常用 media；the press 可统称新闻界。

## 02 · 预料与等待

awaiting。可比较 The audience is waiting for the result.

前三词都可谈预期；await 直接接等待的对象。

## 03 · recreation

The council should provide more space for outdoor recreation.

表示休闲活动这个总称时，通常用不可数名词。

## 04 · 旧报社的三种工作

A messenger brought the invitation to the theatre.

correspondent 常负责特定地区或领域；messenger 负责传递消息或物件。

## 05 · 介绍、解说与评论

The critic wrote a review of the documentary.

announcer 发布消息；commentator 解说或评论；critic 评价作品等。

## 06 · 讲座讲义与活动传单

I received a handout with examples and notes during the talk.

两者都可分发；handout 常用于教学，leaflet 常介绍或宣传活动。

## 07 · 倡导、宣传与达成共识

We reached a consensus on the opening hours of the community centre.

propaganda 常带贬义；reach a consensus 表示达成共识。

## 08 · 发布消息，再扩大传播

Community centres could disseminate event information through local radio.

publish 强调公开发布；disseminate 强调把信息传播给更多人。

## 09 · 公园里的消遣与逗乐

Sketching is my favourite pastime because it helps me slow down.

pastime 是消遣活动；amuse 常用于让人发笑，entertain 的范围更广。

## 10 · 闲聊与传闻

I would check the theatre's official website before sharing the rumour.

gossip 多聊别人的私事；rumour 是未经证实的一条消息。

## 11 · 节庆、盛宴与活动日程

I would include a dance performance in the festival programme.

programme 在此指活动日程；也可以指电视节目。

## 12 · 戏剧排练和登台

At rehearsal, I played the role of a school principal.

play the role of 指扮演某个角色；a scene 是作品中的一个场景。

## 13 · 模仿动作、声音和模拟情境

The training centre can simulate an emergency at sea.

imitate 和 mimic 都可表示模仿；simulate 常指模拟整个情境或过程。

## 14 · 马戏棚的魔术

The audience laughed and clapped after the magic trick.

magic 在此指表演的魔术；a magic trick 是一个魔术把戏。

## 15 · 音乐会、乐团与交响曲

I would like to hear a local orchestra at an outdoor concert.

concert 是活动，orchestra 是演奏者组成的乐团，symphony 是作品。

## 16 · 剧场里看舞、听歌、看悲喜

I would rather see a comedy because I enjoy stories that make me laugh.

ballet、opera 强调表演形式；comedy、tragedy 也可指不同戏剧类型。

## 17 · 影院里的片种和评级

I saw an animation about a boat trying to find its way home.

film 和 movie 都可指电影；X-rated 是旧制或特定制度的成人标示。

## 18 · 艺术家、工艺师与画家

A local craftsman made the wooden box by hand.

painter 可以是 artist；craftsman 强调精湛的制作技艺。

## 19 · 聚光灯下的演出

I heard the guitarist improvise a solo.

improvise 强调现场创作；solo 说明独自演奏。

## 20 · 走进美术馆

I’d like to see a photography exhibition at a local gallery.

gallery 是场所，exhibition 是展出的活动或内容。

## 21 · 策展人怎么选

I’d opt for the photography exhibition.

select 后可直接接对象；opt 常接 for 或 to do。

## 22 · 同一展厅，不同作品

I saw a portrait of a young musician at the museum.

portrait 按题材命名，肖像也可以是一张照片。

## 23 · 画下来，还是说出来？

The mural depicts boats passing beneath a stone bridge.

sketch 强调快速画轮廓；describe 常用语言表达。

## 24 · 音乐节的五种声音

I listen to jazz while cooking and pop on my way to work.

classical 常和 music 连用；音乐类型可以交融。

## 25 · 一首歌的三条线

I like this song because its rhythm makes me want to dance.

整首歌的歌词通常说 lyrics；melody 是曲调，rhythm 是节奏。

## 26 · 录音室的调音与试听

I tuned the guitar before recording the song.

tune a guitar 是调音；a tune 作名词时可以指曲调。

## 27 · 排练室里的四件乐器

I’d rather learn the cello than the violin.

描述演奏时可以说 play the piano 或 play the violin。

## 28 · 街角合奏：吹奏和敲击

Trumpet; harmonica. I heard someone playing the harmonica in the square.

flute 和 trumpet 都用气息吹奏；drum 通常通过敲击发声。

## 29 · 从社区比赛到奥运舞台

I’d like to enter a local tennis tournament.

competition 泛指比赛或竞争；tournament 通常包含多场比赛。

## 30 · 活动背后的三种支持

As a volunteer, I could welcome visitors at the entrance.

sponsor 常用于活动赞助；patron 可指资助艺术的人。

## 31 · 体育场里谁在做什么？

At the stadium, spectators cheered as the runners crossed the finish line.

athlete 是参赛者；spectator 现场观看，不参加比赛。

## 32 · 社区健身房开放日

I like classes with an energetic coach.

famous 和 well-known 都可指出名；vigorous exercise 强调强度。

## 33 · 训练课上的动作和感受

During training, I did a short warm-up exercise before practising my sprint starts.

stretch 是伸展；strain a muscle 指拉伤肌肉。

## 34 · 活动中心的室内与户外

I like indoor yoga and outdoor cycling.

indoor 和 outdoor 修饰名词；indoors 和 outdoors 可以作副词。

## 35 · 舞台上的示意与移动

I motioned for my friend to come over.

motion for someone to do something 表示打手势示意某人做某事。

## 36 · 足球场上的一脚射门

In the last minute, she kicked the ball into the goal.

soccer 在英式英语里常叫 football；goal 也可指进球。

## 37 · 从慢跑热身到马拉松

I usually jog for a few minutes before settling into a steady pace.

keep a steady pace 表示保持稳定的速度。

## 38 · 活动室里的两种桌上游戏

I would like to learn to play chess with my grandfather.

两种活动都可用 play，通常说 play chess 和 play billiards。

## 39 · 三种隔网运动

Can we borrow two badminton rackets?

网球和羽毛球用 racket，排球通常用手或手臂击球。

## 40 · 球具展上看懂不同球杆

At the sports exhibition, a coach showed us a cricket bat.

cricket bat 是板球球板，高尔夫球杆是 golf club。

## 41 · 运动嘉年华的四种手部动作

Toss the beanbag gently into the bucket.

throw 泛指投掷，toss 可强调轻抛，flip 可表示抛硬币。

## 42 · 平稳滑行、打滑与翻滚

She glided across the ice, but her friend slipped near the rail.

glide 强调平稳顺滑，slip 常表示脚下意外打滑。

## 43 · 冬日公园的三条运动路线

I would like to learn to ski on a gentle slope.

ski 和 skate 可直接作动词，cycling 常搭配 go。

## 44 · 入水与随水漂移

The ring drifted away, and the beach ball plunged into the water.

dive 可指有意跳水，plunge 强调突然或用力进入，drift 是随水流移动。

## 45 · 操场上怎样跳

Hop on one foot. If the ball bounces into the grid, pause the game.

jump 用法广，leap 常是大幅跃起，hop 可指单脚跳。

## 46 · 林间露营与野餐

We put up our tent in the morning and had a picnic by the lake.

put up a tent 是搭帐篷，have a picnic 指进行野餐活动。

## 47 · 山林远足寻找线索

We hiked along a forest trail and hunted for clues near the lake.

hunt for 可以是寻找物品或线索，也可用于 hunt for a job。

## 48 · 城市步道里的步子

My brother strode ahead while I lagged behind.

stride 的过去式是 strode，lag behind 指落在后面。

## 49 · 海港假期的一天

I would wander along the shore and linger at a café to watch the sunset.

trip 指一次出行，vacation 指休假的一段时间，英式英语常说 holiday。

## 50 · 从布置舞台到鞠躬谢幕

The actors bowed, and the stage crew dragged the heavy props away.

bow 作鞠躬时读 /baʊ/，表示致意；bend 只说明身体弯曲。
