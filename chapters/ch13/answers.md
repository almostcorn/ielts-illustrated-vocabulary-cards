# 第 13 章「建筑场所」参考答案与用法提醒

答案按卡号排列。开放表达可以使用其他意思相符、表达自然的句子。

## 01 · 沿河步行，找对地点

The venue is situated beside the river, just past the clock tower, a familiar local landmark.

venue 指举办活动的场所。

## 02 · architecture

I like the library's modern architecture, especially the timber arches and large skylight.

architecture 也可指建筑设计这门学科。

## 03 · 工地上正在建什么

The council plans to construct a footbridge over the canal.

架设钢架可说 the erection of a steel frame。

## 04 · 一座社区中心怎样落成

Local volunteers founded the group to offer free art classes.

创办机构可用 found，过去式是 founded。

## 05 · 桥面下的结构与材料

The footbridge has a steel deck resting on concrete supports.

stability 指保持稳固的状态。

## 06 · 地点与居住地怎么说

The library is located near the river.

reside in 后接地点，inhabit 可直接接地点。

## 07 · 从搬迁到安家

Many families migrated to the city and settled near their workplaces.

migrate 强调迁移，settle 强调安定下来。

## 08 · 城市里三类醒目的建筑

A tall glass skyscraper stands out above the old houses.

mansion 强调住宅宏大，villa 常指独立别墅。

## 09 · 长期住还是短住

I'd like a small flat with a balcony and plenty of natural light.

flat 常用于英式英语，apartment 在美式英语中常见。

## 10 · 山间三种小屋

We stayed at a mountain lodge with a view of the valley.

hut 多指简易小屋，log cabin 强调原木搭建。

## 11 · 乡间小屋的四处空间

The cottage has a covered porch and a shed for bikes.

porch 在门外。cellar 在地面以下。

## 12 · 雨天去托儿所

We took shelter from the rain in a bus shelter.

cradle 指摇篮。shelter 也可指避雨处。

## 13 · 俯看棋盘式街区

Walk two blocks and turn left at the library.

two blocks 可表示两个街区的距离。

## 14 · 从接待处走到座位

The corridor leads to the theatre. An aisle separates the two blocks of seats.

aisle 可指成排座位之间的过道。

## 15 · 一级台阶与整座楼梯

Take the staircase to the next floor.

stair 可指一级。stairs 常指整段楼梯。

## 16 · 去楼上怎么走

The two-storey library has an escalator near the entrance.

elevator 在英式英语中常说 lift。

## 17 · 柱子如何托住横梁

The stone pillars support a heavy wooden beam.

pillar 和 column 都可指柱。beam 也可指光束。

## 18 · 拱门、拱顶与天花板

I walked through the arch and looked up at the curved ceiling.

arch 指单个拱。vault 可指覆盖空间的拱顶。

## 19 · 屋檐与屋顶剖面

A brick chimney rises above the roof.

eaves 常以复数形式出现。

## 20 · 在酒店大厅办理入住

I would like to reserve a double room for two nights.

reserve a room 也可以说 book a room。

## 21 · 两种客厅与两种取暖设施

There is a radiator under the window in my living room.

parlour 常见于旧式住宅语境。

## 22 · 厨房里的储物柜

I keep my plates in a cabinet next to the sink.

a kitchen cabinet 指厨房储物柜。

## 23 · 阳台和露台有什么区别

Our room had a small balcony overlooking the sea.

balcony 常突出外墙。terrace 可指宽阔的露天平台。

## 24 · 盥洗室里的三件设施

The toilet is beside the bath.

toilet 在英式英语中也可指厕所。

## 25 · 购物中心里的不同店铺

The booth is near the supermarket.

mall 指整个购物中心，booth 可指一个小摊位。

## 26 · 综合建筑群里的场所

The new complex has an art studio and a small gallery.

complex 作名词可指一组相连或相关的建筑。

## 27 · 从市中心到城市外围

New housing was built in the peripheral areas of the city.

in the vicinity of 表示在某地附近。

## 28 · 宽敞、空处与密封

I would like a spacious room with plenty of natural light.

room 还可表示可用空间，如 room for a table。

## 29 · 古堡前的广场和开阔草地

The plaza in front of the castle has a small fountain.

a vast expanse of 可描述大片开阔地带。

## 30 · 木匠和石匠在修什么

The mason is repairing the stone wall.

brickwork 常指整片砖砌结构。

## 31 · 工地上的钩、杆和脚手架

The hook is to the left of the scaffold.

pole 指长杆。lever 可指控制机器的杆。

## 32 · 桥梁施工暂缓期间

Cities should maintain key infrastructure such as roads and bridges.

suspension 在这里表示暂时停止。

## 33 · 河谷里的两项工程

A new dam was built upstream of the railway viaduct.

span 在这里指相邻桥墩之间的跨度。

## 34 · 社区维修间

The lift malfunctioned, and a technician came to fix it.

maintain 可指日常维护，fix 可指修好故障。

## 35 · 木工台上的调整

I modified the plan to fit two shelves under the window.

modify 指修改，trim 可指修整边缘。

## 36 · 门框工位上的工具

The worker used a drill to make holes in the frame.

plumb line 指带重锤的铅垂线。

## 37 · 布置新公寓

I would furnish the room with a sofa and install a reading light.

assemble 强调把零件组在一起。

## 38 · 图书馆的平面布局

In the new layout, the reading area is closer to the entrance.

layout 看整体，placement 看某件东西放哪儿。

## 39 · 从展馆入口到出口

Go in through the main entrance and use the exit by the garden.

entrance 常指入口，entry 也可指进入权。

## 40 · 建筑外面与向外

The building has a brick exterior and an external staircase.

exterior 指建筑外部，outward 强调方向。

## 41 · 走进建筑内部

The door opens inward, and the inner courtyard is beyond the lobby.

inner courtyard 指内院，internal wall 指内墙。
