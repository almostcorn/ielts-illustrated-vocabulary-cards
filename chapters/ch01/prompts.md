LAYOUT:
Use urban transport reference ONLY for its coherent scene-map architecture, not its palette or city objects. Ocean turquoise, glacial white and slate rock gray provide meaning-based colors. Large title, then ONE continuous beautiful coastline-to-mountain landscape with a SHALLOW LOCAL solid-rock cutaway covering about 60% of the card. The visible rocks are a LOCAL EXAMPLE OF PART OF THE LITHOSPHERE, not a complete cross-section of its entire thickness. Use natural solid rock bands; do not attempt to depict a full crust-and-mantle stack, a crust/mantle boundary, the bottom of the lithosphere or a complete lithosphere extent. No molten layer.

For lithosphere use ONE ORDINARY short leader line with an endpoint on a visible solid rock area. That line points to a representative piece of rock only; it does NOT mark, span or enclose the extent of the whole lithosphere. NO brace, bracket, enclosing contour, full-depth span or lower-boundary marker. Do not treat the entire dark-gray cutaway as the complete lithosphere. Four callouts on quiet ivory fields, not four independent panels. Below the scene use two relationship ribbons, with the lithosphere ribbon reading exactly “地壳＋最上部的刚性地幔 → 岩石圈”. Then the two-English-sentence panel, two compact IELTS application lines and the recall strip. No numerical scale or pretend full-Earth depths. Do not require the local landscape to distinguish or label the mantle. No legend or written labels beyond supplied exact text. Keep the conceptual non-scale note on the illustration edge.
